[REHOOK 0:10] The Schedule Trigger runs on a clock. Choose seconds up to months, or paste a custom cron expression.

[REHOOK 0:17] The catch: a scheduled workflow only runs once you publish it. Saving alone does nothing.

[REHOOK 0:22] Testing by hand is a manual execution. Only production runs count toward your plan quota.

[REHOOK 0:27] Pick the trigger first, then the nodes. Everything else in the workflow depends on how it starts.
