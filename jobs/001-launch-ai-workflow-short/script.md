# 001-launch-ai-workflow-short — script outline

**Topic:** Day 1 of a 7-day AI workflow series. One PDF upload into Google NotebookLM's Studio panel
produces three finished, genuinely-real outputs from the same sources: an Audio Overview podcast, a
Slide Deck, and a Mind Map. First official launch Short for AI Graphic TV, built on the upgraded
high-budget SaaS-launch template (bigger die-cut-sticker HostAvatar, Pain/Shift hook, cinematic
transitions, bespoke custom scenes, no-repeated-scenes validator).

**Why this topic:** matches the "7-Day AI Workflow Series" / "1 Input -> 3 Real Outputs" viral angle
family in `research/config.json`, and every claim checks out against Google's own NotebookLM docs and
2025-2026 feature coverage (see `factmap.md`) -- nothing here is invented or deprecated.

## Scene-by-scene

1. **Hook (pain/shift).** Pain: manually exporting one PDF into three different apps. Shift: one free
   tool now builds all three. HostAvatar `alert` pose docked bottom-right.
2. **Browsersim (app).** A generic AI-tool window: upload one PDF into "Studio panel" (never recreates
   NotebookLM's real UI/logo -- generic chrome per render_guide.md).
3. **Key Twist -- `ThreeOutputsCard` (bespoke).** 1 PDF -> 3 outputs bento (Podcast / Slide deck / Mind
   map), HostAvatar `explaining` pose PiP via the new `HostAvatarPiP` helper.
4. **`AudioWaveCard` (bespoke).** Zoom on output #1: Audio Overview's two-host podcast format.
5. **`SlideStackCard` (bespoke).** Zoom on output #2: Slide Deck, fanned mini-slide stack.
6. **`MindMapCard` (bespoke).** Zoom on output #3: Mind Map, central-node branch diagram.
7. **Dochighlighter.** A short, real, sourced line confirming all three outputs live in the same Studio
   panel (scan-line reveal + VERIFIED stamp).
8. **Datavizcard (bars).** Manual exporting vs Studio panel, qualitative time comparison (not an invented
   stat -- framed as "minutes" vs "an hour", see factmap.md).
9. **Workflowstepper.** 3-step recap: Upload source -> Open Studio -> Pick output.
10. **Toolgrid.** NotebookLM / Audio Overview / Slide Deck named plainly (no logos).
11. **Datavizcard (gauge).** Weekly time-given-back framing, same qualitative basis as beat 8.
12. **Hookcard (second use -- Day 2 tease).** "Day 2 of 7: the mind map becomes a quiz" -- sets up the
    next video in the series without overclaiming what beat 12 alone can prove.
13. **Workflowstepper (7-Day Series Roadmap).** DAY 01 active / DAY 02-07 locked, binge-structure teaser.
14. **Socialctacard (sole closing beat).** Comment-to-DM: "GUIDE" -> Studio Panel Guide (matches
    `guide.json`).
