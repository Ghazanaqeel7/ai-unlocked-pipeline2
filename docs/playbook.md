# AI Unlocked playbook

The rules Claude follows when picking topics and writing titles, hooks and scripts. Claude rewrites this file
every Monday from the research bot's data (`docs/research/`) and our own videos' numbers. The owner can edit it
too.

Confidence labels: **[proven]** = our own videos show it · **[signal]** = the niche shows it (research bot) ·
**[start]** = starting assumption, not tested yet.

_Last updated: 19 Nov 2026 (first version, before any of our videos had data)._

## Topics
- [start] Concrete, immediately usable tricks ("this one ChatGPT setting", "the prompt pattern nobody uses") beat
  vague "AI is changing everything" takes — viewers come to this niche to walk away able to do something new today.
- [start] Cover tools people already have free access to (ChatGPT free/plus, Claude, Gemini, free tiers of
  Midjourney/Perplexity/n8n) more than paid-only or enterprise tools — matches "every type of user" audience.
- [start] Round-up format ("5 tricks", "3 things nobody tells you about X") performs well across the niche's
  biggest channels (@AIGuruOfficialz, @ai_guy, @YourAIGuy09) — numbered lists are scannable and rewatchable.
- [start] "You're doing X wrong" / "nobody tells you this" framing is common in the niche and sets up a clear payoff.

## Titles
- [start] Specific claim + specific tool, ≤ 60 characters (e.g. "This ChatGPT Setting Triples Your Output").
- [start] Numbers in titles ("5 AI Tricks", "3 Prompts") read as concrete and scannable.
- [start] Avoid titles the video can't actually deliver on — broad audience + high expectations means
  under-delivering on a title hurts retention and trust fast.

## Hooks (first 10-15 s)
- [start] State the single most useful/surprising result in the first sentence — never a slow "hey guys welcome
  back" intro.
- [start] Name the tool immediately; explain any term in the same breath it's introduced (broad audience, assume
  no prior knowledge).

## Length and pacing
- [start] 4-8 minutes, organized into numbered chapters/tricks with a `chapter` scene divider between them —
  matches the "scannable, rewatchable" niche pattern and gives YouTube chapter markers to boost retention metrics.
- [start] New visual beat every 4-8 seconds during tricks/hook; slower (8-15s) only for `stat`/`chart` beats.

## What to avoid
- Memes, politics, war, unrelated niches.
- Copying a competitor's script, title or exact angle — topics/tools yes, their words no.
- Stating a deprecated or changed AI-tool feature as currently true — tools change fast, verify against current
  docs/changelog every time.
- Recreating a real tool's exact UI or logo — use the abstract/illustrative motion-graphics style instead.
